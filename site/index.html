Tree trimming information for Scotland property owners

Why Is The Ground Under Scotland So Dry?

This village sits at the tail end of the Galt Moraine. The deposit left behind is sand and gravel through which rain passes almost immediately, and the nearest creek is twelve kilometres away, so nothing in the landscape retains moisture.

Trees up on the crest go through a dry spell every single summer, and tree trimming is how we answer it. Roots run outward instead of downward, since depth offers them nothing, and that single fact should govern how tree trimming is carried out on this ground.

Why Do Dry-Site Trees Recover Slowly From A Heavy Cut?

Regrowth after tree trimming is funded from stored energy, and a tree that goes thirsty each August never accumulates much of a store.

Hence the safe tree trimming percentage up here falls short of what is published for better soils. Overcut a dry-site tree and the response is feeble growth and a specimen worse off than it started.

What Changes Two Concessions Toward Oakland?

Everything changes at Oakland Swamp. The crest is dry sand, but the low ground below it stays wet through most of the year and supports silver maple, willow and ash rather than oak.

Wet soil produces rapid growth, rapid growth produces weak timber, and weak timber fails. Trees in that low ground want tree trimming work at shorter intervals, and for reasons that have nothing to do with the ones three kilometres uphill.

Why Is Oak Trimmed Only In The Dormant Season?

Our tree trimming calendar is set by the oak ridge crossing this area. A fungal disease called oak wilt is fatal to oak, and beetles drawn to fresh sap ferry it from one tree to the next.

Warm weather is the only time those beetles are on the wing. The very same cut in January is entirely safe, and that is why we place all oak tree trimming inside the dormant months.

Why Do The Oldest Trees Here Need Special Care?

A number of the trees in Scotland went into the ground in the 1840s, and people are genuinely fond of them. Removal is not what anybody wants, and usually it is not what should happen.

Tree trimming, specifically a canopy reduction and sometimes steel alongside it, is what an old tree normally needs. Compared with felling, the cost is small, and the result is that something which cannot be replaced stays where it is.

What Does Tree Trimming Cost In Scotland?

A tree trimming price comes down to three variables. The first is the size of the tree. The second is what occupies the ground below it. The third is how close a vehicle can be brought to the stem.

Cheapest of all tree trimming is a tree at a field edge with nothing but grass below, and Scotland has no shortage of those. Take that same tree to a Simcoe Street lot with a garage alongside and cables above, and the tree trimming cost multiplies. The figure is committed to paper only after somebody has been beneath the tree.

When Is Tree Removal The Right Answer?

Tree removal is justified on three grounds. The tree has died, it has a structural defect no cut will remedy, or it has simply become too large for where it was put.

A good number of the trees we are asked to take down will carry on quite happily with tree trimming instead. When that is the case you will be told, and no obligation follows.

Our Process

What Happens During A Tree Trimming Visit?

  1. Any tree trimming visit opens with a survey of the whole property. Trees set out along one fence line in a single season generally develop matching defects, so the tree that prompted your call is rarely the only candidate.

  2. The next decision is the tree trimming method for each limb. Grass underneath means a section can simply be cut free and allowed to drop. Shingles, fencing or a parked vehicle underneath means rigging lines instead.

  3. When the tree trimming ends the brush is chipped, the larger wood is either piled up or loaded out according to your instructions, and the ground is raked over. An improved tree ought to be the only evidence we were there.
