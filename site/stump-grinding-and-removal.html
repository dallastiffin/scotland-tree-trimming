Stump Grinding and Removal details

How Does Stump Grinding Work?

Stump grinding turns a cutting wheel fitted with hardened teeth at speed and takes the stump apart in thin slices, moving lower and wider with each pass until nothing stands above the lawn.

By default we leave the resulting chip in place to fill the hole, with soil spread over the top.

Why Is Stump Grinding Straightforward On This Ground?

Few materials treat stump grinding and removal as gently as sand does. Cutting is fast, teeth stay sharp, and progress is even throughout the job.

The price reflects that. Delays on this ground almost always trace back to one of three things: a stone under the grass, fencing wire the tree has grown around, or a buried line nobody had recorded.

How Deep Should A Stump Be Ground?

What happens to that ground afterwards sets the depth, and we commit to a figure on the quote:

- Half a foot under the surface is plenty for grass, whose roots never go far down - More than that where planting is going in, so new roots find earth and not compacted chip - Deeper still under paving, a shed base or posts, chasing the lateral roots out as we go - Barely below grade, and cheapest of all, when the only aim is to save the mower blades

Why Does The Backfill Settle So Quickly?

Backfill from stump grinding packs down more rapidly than heavier soils do, so you will notice the dip inside a few months instead of after a full year.

Mound the fill slightly above the surrounding grass and budget for topping it up once. Plants going into that spot need thorough watering for two seasons, because loose backfill sheds water even quicker than the sand beside it.

What Should I Mention When I Call?

Mentioning these three at the outset avoids surprises on the stump grinding and removal price:

- Anything running underground, because the cutting wheel reaches pipe and cable at full revolutions - The gap at the entrance, since only the smaller grinder passes through a normal gateway - Stone or wire grown into the timber, which will strip a wheel of its teeth
