What Should I Tell You When I Call?

Supply these five details and the tree trimming quote should be right without a follow-up:

- Identification of the tree, with its height compared against a rooftop or similar landmark - Whereabouts the property sits, in the village, on a concession, or in one of the hamlets - Whatever lies beneath the canopy, be that shingles, gravel, fence, cable or plain lawn - The problem as you see it, such as a sparse crown, a caught limb, or a lean that was not there before - Entrance width, and whether a vehicle can turn around once it is on the property

What Happens After I Get In Touch?

Tree trimming pricing follows inspection, never precedes it, so the first step is agreeing a time to come out. That attendance is free and obliges you to nothing.

Then comes the tree trimming quote in writing. One line per tree, showing the planned work, where brush and wood are going, and anything excluded. If a line puzzles you, put the question to us.

What If A Tree Is On A Hydro Line?

Contact the electricity provider first. Ownership of that problem rests with them until the conductor is confirmed dead, and nothing should be cut beforehand.

Until then, treat every object in contact with the tree as energised, fences, gates, equipment and cars alike.
